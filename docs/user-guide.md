# AgriOptima AI — User Guide

## Fast 2-Minute Judging Walkthrough
1. **Launch App**: Open the live application.
2. **Click "Try Demo"**: The amber button at the top header immediately populates *GreenAcres Sustainable Farm* (15 hectares, Loam soil, 85k m³ water, $12k budget).
3. **Inspect Top Cards**: Review Land utilization, Water budget stress, Capital expenditure, Top AI Crop, Expected Production, Projected Profit, and Risk Level.
4. **Digital Farm Spatial Map**: View proportional parcels dynamically partitioned across allocated crops with irrigation canal indicators.
5. **AI Crop Suitability**: Switch to "AI Crop Suitability" tab to view ranked recommendations and toggle between "Farmer Explanation" (plain language pros/cons) and "Technical Breakdown" (pH curves and water indices).
6. **What-If Simulator (Main Feature)**: Switch to "What-If Simulator" tab.
   - Click one-click preset: **"Water Shortage (-40%)"**.
   - Click **"RECALCULATE SCENARIO"**.
   - Observe live Before vs After comparisons: Profit shift, water savings, harvest delta, and dynamic crop mix shifts.
7. **Download PDF Report**: Click "PDF Report" in header to generate and download an executive farm decision summary.
